Shark Attacks in Australia

Where do shark attacks happen?

Australia has one of the highest rates of shark attacks in the world. New South Wales and Queensland — the most densely populated coastal states — record the greatest number of incidents. The map below shows the distribution of shark attacks across all Australian states and territories.

Which sharks are most dangerous?

Not all sharks pose the same level of threat. White Sharks are responsible for the most attacks overall, while Tiger Sharks and Bull Sharks also feature prominently. What were the victims doing when attacked?

The activity a person is engaged in significantly influences their risk of a shark attack. Swimming is by far the most common activity at the time of attack, followed by boarding (surfing and bodyboarding). Understanding high-risk activities can help beachgoers make more informed decisions about water safety.

Which body parts are most at risk?

Shark attacks most commonly target the legs, which is consistent with activities like swimming and surfing where legs are most exposed.
